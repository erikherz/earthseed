Nothing here has been verified, and nothing acts on its own. We cannot decrypt a stream, so every report is an anonymous claim by someone who held a share link. A count is not evidence — a single person with a grudge and an invitation can file repeatedly. Terminate because you judged it right, not because the number grew. A frame is the one thing here that shows rather than asserts, and it is still only what a reporter's browser handed us for a single instant: it can be pointed at the wrong moment, and it cannot tell you what came before or after.
