Private live streaming

Go live from your camera and share a link so anyone with it can watch. No account, no install. Your video is encrypted in your browser — the relay that carries it only ever sees ciphertext it can't read.

Broadcast Watch

Small enough to read

Each page loads exactly two pieces of JavaScript, on purpose:

  • Our whole client — earthseed.js: one unminified, documented file that runs in the browser as-is (no build step). It does capture, encode, encrypt, decrypt, decode and render. What you read is what runs.
  • The transport — @moq/net (Media over QUIC), loaded directly from its published package at a pinned version. We don't vendor or modify it — you can verify it upstream.

No accounts, no analytics, no server-side list of streams.

How the key works

Each broadcast has a content key derived in your browser. The random part lives only in the #… fragment of the share link — browsers never send a URL fragment to a server — so no relay or host can derive it. Whoever has the link can watch; nobody else can decrypt.
